Featured Trip Packages

Los Dientes del Diablo (Full Day)

Difficulty: Advanced (Class IV-V)

Experience the ultimate adrenaline rush tackling the challenging rapids of Los Dientes del Diablo. Perfect for experienced thrill-seekers looking for an action-packed day on the river, led by our elite senior guides.

Half-Day Scenic Splash

Difficulty: Beginner / Intermediate (Class II-III)

Ideal for families and first-time rafters! This scenic trip offers a wonderful balance of gentle floating stretches and fun, manageable splashes through stunning canyon landscapes.

Multi-Day Canyon Expedition

Difficulty: Intermediate (Class III-IV)

Immerse yourself completely in nature with our 3-day backcountry expedition. Camp under the stars, enjoy riverside meals cooked by our guides, and conquer miles of pristine whitewater.
